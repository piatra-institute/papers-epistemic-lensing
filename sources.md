## Structured bibliography (2026-09-23)

The inline reference list was migrated to references.yaml (CSL) and in-text citations to Pandoc [@id] syntax. All entries except Pariser were resolved through doi.org content negotiation and checked for year, title and authors: Friston 2013 (10.1098/rsif.2013.0475), Friston et al. 2017 (10.1162/neco_a_00912), Shannon (10.1002/j.1538-7305.1948.tb01338.x), Tversky and Kahneman (10.1126/science.185.4157.1124), Lewandowsky et al. (10.1177/1529100612451018), Walter et al. (10.1080/10584609.2019.1668894), Benkler, Faris and Roberts (10.1093/oso/9780190923624.001.0001) and Pearl (10.1016/C2009-0-27609-4). The automatic Crossref match for Pariser was a book review, so the book was entered by hand. Kahneman and Tversky (1979) was listed in the references but cited nowhere in the text and supports no statement in it, so it was removed. The Bernstein et al. (2018) and Sunstein (2001) entries in the list above are not cited in the paper and are not part of references.yaml. Source claims in claims.yaml were checked against abstracts retrieved from Crossref or OpenAlex.
